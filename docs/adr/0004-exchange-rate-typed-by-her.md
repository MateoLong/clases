# The USD Cotización is typed by her, not fetched

The owner chose this (2026-10-03): it works offline, needs no outside service, and never changes behind her back. A Cotización is always read with its comma or dot as decimals ("39,875" is 39.875 pesos), and values outside 1–1000 are refused. Elsewhere, amounts follow the Uruguayan convention: dots group thousands, the comma is the decimal mark.
