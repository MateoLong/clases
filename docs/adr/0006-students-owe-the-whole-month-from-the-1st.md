# Students owe the whole month's Cuota from the 1st; a missed Clase is charged

The owner told us (2026-10-04) how she really charges: families pay at the start of the month for the whole month, and a Clase the student misses is still charged. So what an Alumno owes is no longer "the Clases already given" (ADR 0002) but the **Cuota** of every month that has started: all their billable Clases dated in that month, each at the Tarifa of its date. Te deben = Cuotas so far − Pagos, so "Cobrar" on the 1st collects the month in one tap.

A cancelled Clase now says why. **Faltó** (the student didn't come) stays charged. **Suspendida** (she called it off) leaves the Cuota. If that happens after the month was paid, the student has money **a favor**, which lowers the next month. Pagos aren't tied to a month: they pay the oldest Cuotas first, and that is how the student page splits "Pagado de este mes" from "De meses anteriores".

What does **not** change: Ganado, which is what Ganancias reports, still counts a Clase when it ends (ADR 0002), so the charts don't jump on the 1st, and rates and slots still change only from a date (ADR 0003). Te deben now moves when she changes a Clase still to come this month (a new Clase extra, a suspension, archiving). That is intended, because the Cuota follows the agenda. Old cancels have no reason saved, and they keep their meaning: charged ones read as Faltó, uncharged ones as Suspendida.

Rejected: a flat monthly fee per student, because she prices by the hour and months have 4 or 5 weeks. Also rejected: a per-student choice between paying by the month and paying by the Clase. Nobody asked for it, and a student without a fixed day fits the Cuota too: their Cuota is the Clases booked for them that month.
