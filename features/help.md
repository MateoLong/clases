# Cómo se usa
Path: the round "?" in the top band (any screen), or "cómo se usa" in the first-run welcome → `#/ayuda`.
End state: eight step-by-step cards (semana, cambiar, extra, cobrar, alumnos, ganancias, copia, probar); each "Ir a…" button opens that screen. Reading it writes nothing to the device.
Empty lists say what to do next: a student with no weekly day and no upcoming classes points to "Días de clase"; Ganancias with no students points to Alumnos.
