# Ganancias y ¿Y si…?
Ganancias: tiles (this month, this week, average of the last full months, owed). Columns by month (6 back + next) or by week (8 back + next), split into ganado and agendado, with a tooltip on hover or focus and a table view. This month by student.
¿Y si…?: steppers for new students and classes per week, chips for length, a slider for the rate. The extra per month, week and year, the new total and the hours update instantly and match `registry.projection()`.
Currency: the band switch flips every amount between UYU and USD using the rate set in Ajustes.
