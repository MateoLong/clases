# Agenda
Path: Agenda (opens on this week, today highlighted) → ‹ › to change week, "Hoy" to come back.
End state: each weekly slot appears as a label on its day. A label is "dada" once its end time has passed. Tapping a label selects it, and the rail then offers Faltó (charged) / La suspendo yo (not charged; "Cobrarla igual" afterwards) / Mover / Volver a su día. A Faltó label is tinted and says "faltó"; a suspended one is struck out and says "suspendida". Every action can be undone from the toast. "Clase extra" adds a one-off class. The rail shows what she has earned this week, what is still to come, the week total and hours, and "Te deben" (each month's whole Cuota is owed from the 1st, ADR 0006), where each student has a Cobrar button that stamps PAGADO and saves a payment for exactly what is owed, so on the 1st it collects the month. Students with money a favor are not listed.
Planificación: the selected Clase has a Planificación box; saving it marks the label with a pencil, it follows the Clase through Mover / Cancelar / Volver a su día, it is listed under that Clase on the student page, and it can be cleared or undone. It never changes money.
A Clase extra or a moved Clase that would overlap another Clase that day is refused, naming who it clashes with; a cancelled Clase leaves its time free.
