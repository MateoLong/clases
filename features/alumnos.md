# Alumnos
Path: Alumnos → Agregar alumno (name, rate per class in UYU or USD, whatever the class lasts, optional "Datos del colegio": Colegio, Año 1º–6º, Maestra/o and mail; optional weekly day / time / length) → student page.
End state: student and slot saved. The student page shows one part at a time, picked with the chips under the name (Cuenta · Días de clase · Tarifa · Próximas clases). It opens on Cuenta, and the open part stays open through the redraw after a change; another student starts on Cuenta. The pencil next to the name opens Datos (name, school details, notes) above the chips; Guardar closes it, a refused mail keeps it open, Cancelar closes it unchanged. Notes show under the name. Overlapping slots are refused, naming who they clash with. On the student page: register a payment (full or partial); change the rate from a date (classes before keep the old rate; under Tarifa, the list of Tarifas has a ✕ on all but the first, which deletes one typed by mistake so its dates go back to the one before, with Deshacer; a rate saved before 2026-10-17 shows as "por hora" until then, ADR 0007); add, change (from a date) or remove days; edit name, school details and notes with the pencil; archive (future classes stop, debts and history stay). School details show under the name (the mail is a mailto link), the Año shows in the list, all four are editable with the pencil and exported to Excel.
Sin día fijo: leaving the day as "Sin día fijo" saves no weekly slot (an hour typed without a day is refused). Every student page has "Agendar clase" (date, time, length; starts on 50 min and on the first day without a Clase of theirs), the main button for students sin día fijo; a clash is refused naming who. Their page points to Agendar clase instead of asking for a day.
Cuenta: what they owe (or have a favor), plus this month: Cuota (with its number of Clases), paid toward it, what's left or a favor, and anything left from earlier months.
