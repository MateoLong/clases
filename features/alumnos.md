# Alumnos
Path: Alumnos → Agregar alumno (name, rate per hour in UYU or USD, optional "Datos del colegio": Colegio, Año 1º–6º, Maestra/o and mail; optional weekly day / time / length) → student page.
End state: student and slot saved. Overlapping slots are refused, naming who they clash with. On the student page: register a payment (full or partial); change the rate from a date (classes before keep the old rate); add, change (from a date) or remove days; edit name and notes; archive (future classes stop, debts and history stay). School details show under the name (the mail is a mailto link), the Año shows in the list, all four are editable under Datos and exported to Excel.
Sin día fijo: leaving the day as "Sin día fijo" saves no weekly slot (an hour typed without a day is refused). Every student page has "Agendar clase" (date, time, length; starts on 50 min and on the first day without a Clase of theirs), the main button for students sin día fijo; a clash is refused naming who. Their page points to Agendar clase instead of asking for a day.
