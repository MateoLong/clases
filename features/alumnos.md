# Alumnos
Path: Alumnos → Agregar alumno (name, rate per hour in UYU or USD, optional weekly day / time / length) → student page.
End state: student and slot saved. Overlapping slots are refused, naming who they clash with. On the student page: register a payment (full or partial); change the rate from a date (classes before keep the old rate); add, change (from a date) or remove days; edit name and notes; archive (future classes stop, debts and history stay).
